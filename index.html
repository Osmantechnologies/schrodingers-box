01

The experiment

Not an animation of a cat state — an actual one, evaluated on a grid every frame and drawn from the numbers it produces.

01 / STATE

A real cat state

The chamber holds an equal superposition of N coherent states arranged on a ring in phase space. Separation, squeeze, phase and the number of components are yours to set; the state is rebuilt from them.

|ψ⟩ = 𝒩 · S(r) · Σₖ |α e^(i(2πk/N + φ))⟩
02 / EVIDENCE

Wigner negativity

The distribution over phase space is computed on a 96×96 grid, cross terms kept. Where it goes negative, no classical probability distribution can follow it. That negativity is the proof the box holds a superposition and not a mixture.

∫W<0 |W| d²β  →  1/π for a two-lobe cat
03 / COLLAPSE

Measurement, honestly

Pressing observe draws one sample from the marginal P(x) — the real distribution, not a coin flip over two labels. Repeat it two hundred times and the histogram converges to the curve it came from.

x ~ P(x) = ∫ W(x,p) dp
02

Field notes

Why this cat, and why 2023.

I tried liking our cat, but the feeling wasn't reciprocated. Not even slightly. One day she disappeared. To this day, we don't know if our cat (Schrödinger), is alive or dead. Elon Musk · 17 Sep 2023 · x.com

A cat named Schrödinger walked off and never resolved. It is the only Schrödinger's cat anyone has actually had to live with — an unresolved state with a name, a food bowl and a household that stopped waiting.

Three years later, a group in Oxford showed that the thought experiment had been too modest all along. A superposition does not have to be two of the same thing in two places. Its parts can be things with no classical version at all. The box was never a question with two answers.

  • 1935Schrödinger publishes the cat — as an argument against taking superposition literally.
  • 1996Monroe, Meekhof, King & Wineland build the first real cat state from a single trapped ion.
  • 2018"We have a big dog called Gatsby, a little dog called Marvin the Martian & a cat named Schrödinger."
  • 2023She disappears. State unresolved.
  • 2026Oxford builds cat states whose components are themselves non-classical — squeezed, trisqueezed, quadsqueezed. Sixfold symmetry, Wigner negativity confirmed.
03

Methodology

What is computed and what is drawn. The line between them is the whole point.

Computed from the state

  • Wigner function — 96×96 grid (72² on phones), closed-form coherent-state overlaps, cross terms kept.
  • Negativity, purity, fidelity — numerical integrals over that grid. A two-component cat returns 0.3175; the textbook value is 1/π = 0.318.
  • Photon statistics — ⟨n⟩, parity and P(n) from the Fock amplitudes.
  • Decoherence — coherences damped by the photon-loss form, so distant branches lose their fringes first.
  • Observation — a Born-rule sample from P(x), logged and histogrammed.

Illustrative

  • The cat — a generated 3D mesh rendered as a hologram: wireframe, Fresnel shell, and particles sampled from its surface.
  • Its copies — placed by the same α, φ, N and κ that drive the physics, but they are a picture of the state, not the state.
  • Breathing, head and tail motion — procedural. The model has no rig.
  • The chamber — grids, scan plane and wavefront rings are scene dressing.
