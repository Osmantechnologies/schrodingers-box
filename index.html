01 / STATE

A real cat state

An equal superposition of N coherent states on a ring in phase space. The lobes you see are the components; the ripples between them are interference, and they only exist because the cross terms are kept.

|ψ⟩ = 𝒩 · S(r) · Σₖ |α e^(i(2πk/N + φ))⟩
02 / EVIDENCE

Wigner negativity

Push κ up and watch the fringes die while the lobes survive. That is decoherence: the parts stay, the betweenness goes. Negativity falls to zero and the state becomes an ordinary mixture.

∫W<0 |W| d²β  →  1/π for a two-lobe cat

Field notes

Why this cat, and why 2023.

I tried liking our cat, but the feeling wasn't reciprocated. Not even slightly. One day she disappeared. To this day, we don't know if our cat (Schrödinger), is alive or dead. Elon Musk · 17 Sep 2023 · x.com

A cat named Schrödinger walked off and never resolved. It is the only Schrödinger's cat anyone has had to actually live with — an unresolved state with a name, a food bowl, and a household that stopped waiting.

  • 1935Schrödinger publishes the cat — as an argument against taking superposition literally.
  • 1996Monroe, Meekhof, King & Wineland build the first real cat state from a single trapped ion.
  • 2018"We have a big dog called Gatsby, a little dog called Marvin the Martian & a cat named Schrödinger."
  • 2023She disappears. State unresolved.
  • 2026Oxford builds cat states whose components are themselves non-classical. Sixfold symmetry, Wigner negativity confirmed.
03

Methodology

What is computed and what is drawn. The line between them is the whole point.

Computed from the state

  • Wigner function — 96×96 grid (72² on phones), closed-form coherent-state overlaps, cross terms kept.
  • Negativity, purity, fidelity — numerical integrals over that grid. A two-component cat returns 0.3175; the textbook value is 1/π = 0.318.
  • Photon statistics — ⟨n⟩, parity and P(n) from the Fock amplitudes.
  • Decoherence — coherences damped by the photon-loss form, so distant branches lose their fringes first.
  • Observation — a Born-rule sample from P(x), logged and histogrammed.

Illustrative

  • The cat — a generated 3D mesh rendered as a hologram: wireframe, Fresnel shell, particles sampled from its surface.
  • Its copies — placed by the same α, φ, N and κ that drive the physics, but they are a picture of the state, not the state.
  • Breathing, head and tail motion — procedural. The model has no rig.
  • The chamber and these backdrops — grids, scan plane, rings and generated imagery are scene dressing.

Generating Arbitrary Superpositions of Nonclassical Quantum Harmonic Oscillator States

S. Saner, O. Băzăvan, D. J. Webb, G. Araneda, D. M. Lucas, C. J. Ballance, R. Srinivas — Phys. Rev. X 16, 021049 (2026).
