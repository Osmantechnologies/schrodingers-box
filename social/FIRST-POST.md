## Long version

**Open Schrödinger's box.**

The cat is 91 years old. Schrödinger wrote it in 1935 as a complaint, a joke about taking superposition literally, and the world memorised it backwards: a question with two answers. Since September 2023 a real cat named Schrödinger has been missing; her owner wrote "we don't know if our cat is alive or dead," and the post is still up. In June, Oxford rebuilt the thought experiment in a single trapped ion, and the two-answer version did not survive.

Saner et al., Phys. Rev. X 16, 021049: superpositions sculpted from squeezed, trisqueezed and quadsqueezed components. Sixfold symmetry. Wigner negativity confirmed. In the lead author's words, "a tool to sculpt the quantum superposition into almost any shape." We asked a smaller question: why does everyone know the box, and nobody gets to open one?

**What exists.** One strontium ion in vacuum, its motion steered by a mid-circuit measurement into superpositions whose parts have no classical counterpart at all. The signature is the Wigner function: where it goes negative, no classical story can follow. Sixfold symmetry means six components, not two. Beside the paper sits the folk cat, alive-or-dead, in every textbook and every meme, with no public apparatus anywhere that lets a person hold a cat state and watch it.

**What we built.** Schrödinger's Box, an observation chamber in the browser. The chamber holds a cat state you sculpt: how far apart her branches sit, how many there are, how fast the world leaks in. The Wigner function is computed from the state as you work; the ripples between the lobes are the interference, and where they dip negative you are looking at the part of her that has no classical version. Observing draws one outcome from the state's own distribution. The Born rule, not a coin flip. A study log re-runs six experiments against published theory on every visit, reproducible from a seed, and a two-lobe cat's negativity lands on the textbook 1/π. The repo is open, because an experiment you cannot inspect is a demo.

**How a look works.** Open the box. The cat is in six places, fringes between them. Slide decoherence and watch the betweenness die while the places survive; that is why real cats never superpose. Press OBSERVE. One branch remains, drawn from the distribution you were just looking at. Reset, and she is everywhere again.

**Why this way.** The binary was never the physics. It was an assumption packed into the box in 1935 and never taken back out: a superposition is not two options, it is inhabited space between outcomes, and its occupants owe the classical world nothing. Oxford showed that in an ion. The box shows it to anyone with a browser. Next comes hardware: the same cat prepared on a superconducting processor, parity fringes counting her components, the protocol written and waiting for its run.

Sources: Saner, Băzăvan, Webb, Araneda, Lucas, Ballance, Srinivas, Phys. Rev. X 16, 021049 (2026). Zurek, Nature 412, 712 (2001). Lieb, Commun. Math. Phys. 62, 35 (1978). E. Musk on X, 17 September 2023. github.com/Osmantechnologies/schrodingers-box.
