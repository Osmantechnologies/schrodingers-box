## 02 — The thesis

> Schrödinger's cat is told as a binary: alive or dead.
>
> Oxford's cat states have components that are themselves non-classical. Sixfold symmetry, Wigner negativity confirmed.
>
> The binary was never the physics. It was the assumption. The box holds as many answers as you can sculpt.
